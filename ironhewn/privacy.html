Privacy Policy

Ironhewn · Last updated October 20, 2026

Ironhewn is made by Oddwright, operated by Genaro Gonzalez (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the Ironhewn app collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions: oddwright.support@gmail.com.

The short version. Your training log lives on your phone. If you create an account, it’s backed up to our servers so you can restore it or use it on another phone. Health data from Health Connect stays on your phone. The AI coach only receives your data if you agree to it. We don’t sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or advertising tools.

What we collect and why

Data you enter in the app

  • Training data: workouts, sets, exercises, routines, programs, personal records, ranks, XP and achievements.
  • Profile: name, sex (used for strength standards), bodyweight, height, birth year (optional), units and goals; body measurements if you log them.

This is stored on your device. It’s used to run the app: logging, ranks, progress charts and recommendations.

Account (optional)

If you create an account, we collect your email address to sign you in, and we store a copy of your training data and profile on our servers to back it up and sync it between your devices. Coach conversations and health data are not synced.

Health Connect (optional)

If you connect Health Connect, Ironhewn can read sleep, steps, active calories, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, weight and other apps’ workouts, and can write your Ironhewn workouts, active calories and weigh-ins. We use this to show your daily readiness score, your health summary and recovery insights, and to keep your bodyweight up to date for ranks.

  • Health data stays on your device. It isn’t uploaded to our servers or synced.
  • It’s shared with the AI coach only if you turn on “Include health data” (see below), and then only as a short summary.
  • We never use health data for advertising, sell it, or use it to make decisions about credit, insurance or employment.
  • Ironhewn’s use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI coach (optional)

The coach is an AI model, Claude, provided by Anthropic, PBC. Before your first chat we ask for your permission. If you agree, each message you send includes:

  • a summary of your training (workouts, sets, PRs, ranks and programs) and profile (age, sex, height, bodyweight);
  • the messages you write;
  • a short health summary (sleep, steps, heart rate), only if you turn that on.

Messages go through our servers to Anthropic. Our servers don’t store your messages or the coach’s replies; we keep only usage records (date, model, amount of processing) to enforce plan limits. Anthropic doesn’t use this data to train its models and normally deletes it within 30 days (longer only if flagged for safety review, per Anthropic’s policies). Your coach chats are stored on your device. You can turn off sharing anytime in Settings → AI coach.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Google Play (or the App Store). We never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status, which receives purchase records and an anonymous account ID. When you buy, we keep a record of the price and terms you were shown, as required by consumer-protection laws.

Reports

If you report a coach reply, we receive the reason and the excerpt you chose to send, and keep it for up to 180 days to improve safety.

What we don’t collect

No advertising IDs, no third-party analytics, no location, no contacts, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

How long we keep data

  • Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Account data stays until you delete your account. Deletion is immediate on our servers and removed from backups within 30 days.
  • Purchase consent records: kept for at least 3 years (or 1 year after your subscription ends, if longer), as required by California law.
  • Coach usage records: kept while your account exists. Reports: up to 180 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: export your workouts anytime (Settings → Data → Export CSV, or a full backup).
  • Correct: edit your data in the app.
  • Delete: delete your account and its data in the app (Settings → Account → Delete account) or see Delete your account. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.
  • AI and health sharing: turn either off anytime in Settings → AI coach.
  • California residents: you have the right to know, delete and correct your personal information, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. We don’t sell or share personal information. To make a request, email us.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest on our servers. No method is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you as required by law if a breach affects it.

Age

Ironhewn is for people 18 and older. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used Ironhewn, contact us and we’ll delete the data.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the changes are significant.
