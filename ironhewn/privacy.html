Privacy Policy

Ironhewn · Last updated October 22, 2026

Ironhewn is made by Oddwright, operated by Genaro Gonzalez (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the Ironhewn app collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions: oddwright.support@gmail.com.

The short version. Your training log lives on your phone. If you create an account, it’s backed up to our servers so you can restore it or use it on another phone. Health data from Health Connect stays on your phone. The AI coach only receives your data if you agree to it. If you join leagues, other lifters see your username and scores, never your body stats, health data or workout details. We don’t sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or advertising tools.

What we collect and why

Data you enter in the app

  • Training data: workouts, sets, exercises, routines, programs, personal records, ranks, XP and achievements.
  • Profile: name, sex (used for strength standards), bodyweight, height, birth year (optional), units and goals; body measurements if you log them.

This is stored on your device. It’s used to run the app: logging, ranks, progress charts and recommendations.

Account (optional)

If you create an account, we collect your email address to sign you in. You can sign in with an emailed code, with Google, or on iPhone with Apple:

  • Google tells us your email address, name and profile picture. We store them with your account, but use only the email address.
  • Apple tells us your email address. If you choose “Hide My Email”, it’s a private Apple relay address that forwards to your real one.

We store a copy of your training data, profile and settings on our servers to back it up and sync it between your devices. We also store a random ID for each phone you sign in on and your time zone, to keep your devices in sync and show dates correctly. Coach conversations and health data are not synced.

Leagues and the strength board (optional, needs an account)

Weekly leagues and the strength board are the only places other Ironhewn users can see anything about you.

  • Username: to take part you pick a username. Other lifters see it instead of your name or email. Usernames go through a name filter, and a username reported by several lifters is hidden (shown as “Lifter”) until we review it.
  • Weekly leagues (on by default once you have a username): the lifters in your league see your username, your league tier and your weekly Heat, a score we calculate on our servers from the workouts you log live in the app. Imported or backfilled workouts don’t count.
  • Strength board (off until you turn it on): your username, Forge Score and rank can appear on the board, which all lifters can see. When you join, we lock the sex, height and birth year used to pick your standards category; other lifters don’t see them.
  • Never shown to other users: your email, name, bodyweight, height, age, body measurements, health data, workout details or training times.
  • Ghost rivals: when a league isn’t full, some seats are filled by simulated competitors. They’re always labelled “Ghost”, aren’t real people, and never take a promotion spot from a real lifter.
  • Blocking and reporting: if you block someone, neither of you sees the other in leagues or on the board. If you report a lifter, we store the reason, the username you saw and any note you add, and use it to review the report.

You can hide yourself from leagues or leave the strength board anytime: Ranks → League → settings (the gear icon).

Health Connect (optional)

If you connect Health Connect, Ironhewn can read sleep, steps, active calories, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, weight and other apps’ workouts, and can write your Ironhewn workouts, active calories and weigh-ins. We use this to show your daily readiness score, your health summary and recovery insights, and to keep your bodyweight up to date for ranks.

  • Health data stays on your device. It isn’t uploaded to our servers or synced.
  • Weight is the one exception, and only if you turn it on. With “Use weigh-ins from Health Connect” on, new weigh-ins update the bodyweight in your profile. From then on it’s treated like the rest of your profile: backed up with your account and included in your coach summary.
  • It’s shared with the AI coach only if you turn on “Include health data” (see below), and then only as a short summary.
  • We never use health data for advertising, sell it, or use it to make decisions about credit, insurance or employment.
  • Ironhewn’s use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI coach (optional)

The coach runs on an AI model provided by Anthropic, PBC. Before your first chat we ask for your permission. If you agree, each message you send includes:

  • a summary of your training (workouts, sets, PRs, ranks and programs) and profile (age, sex, height, bodyweight);
  • the messages you write;
  • a short health summary (sleep, steps, calories, heart rate, HRV, workouts from other apps and your readiness score), only if you turn that on.

Messages go through our servers to Anthropic. Our servers don’t store your messages or the coach’s replies; we keep only usage records (date, model, amount of processing) to enforce plan limits. Anthropic doesn’t use this data to train its models and normally deletes it within 30 days (longer only if flagged for safety review, per Anthropic’s policies). Your coach chats are stored on your device. You can turn off sharing anytime in Settings → Coach.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Google Play (or the App Store). We never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. It receives purchase records and an ID: a random one created when the app first opens, replaced by your account ID once you sign in. When you buy, we keep a record of the price and terms you were shown, as required by consumer-protection laws. If you later delete your account, that record is kept without your email or account ID (linked only to a one-way code) and deleted after the legal retention period.

Reports

If you report a coach reply, we receive the reason and the excerpt you chose to send, and keep it for up to 180 days to improve safety. Reports about other lifters are described under Leagues above.

Emails

We email you sign-in codes and, if you start a free trial, a reminder before it turns into a paid subscription. These are sent through Google’s Gmail service from our support address.

What we don’t collect

No advertising IDs, no third-party analytics, no location, no contacts, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

How long we keep data

  • Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Account data stays until you delete your account. Deletion is immediate on our servers and removed from backups within 30 days.
  • Purchase consent records: kept for 3 years (or 1 year after your subscription ends, if longer), as required by California law, including after you delete your account, without your email or account ID.
  • Coach usage records: kept while your account exists. Coach reply reports: up to 180 days.
  • League results, board scores, your username, blocks and reports about other lifters: kept while your account exists, and deleted with it. A report is also deleted if the reported lifter deletes their account.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: export your workouts anytime (Settings → Data → Export CSV, or a full backup).
  • Correct: edit your data in the app.
  • Delete: delete your account and its data in the app (Settings → Account → Delete account) or see Delete your account. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.
  • AI and health sharing: turn either off anytime in Settings → Coach.
  • Leagues and the strength board: hide yourself, leave the board, change your username or unblock lifters in Ranks → League → settings.
  • California residents: you have the right to know, delete and correct your personal information, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. We don’t sell or share personal information. To make a request, email us.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest on our servers. No method is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you as required by law if a breach affects it.

Age

Ironhewn is for people 18 and older. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used Ironhewn, contact us and we’ll delete the data.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the changes are significant.
