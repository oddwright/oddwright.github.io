Restore Pro on a new phone

Sign in with the same account, then open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases.

Cancel or manage your subscription

Google Play → Account → Payments & subscriptions. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Move your workouts to a new phone

Sign in with your account and your history syncs automatically. Without an account, use Settings → Data → Share backup file, then Restore backup on the new phone.

Health Connect

Make sure your health app (Samsung Health, Google Fit, Fitbit and others) is allowed to share with Health Connect, then check Settings → Health Connect in Ironhewn.
