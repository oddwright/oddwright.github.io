Delete your account

Ironhewn by Oddwright

In the app (fastest)

  • Open Ironhewn → Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • Confirm with the code we email you. Your account and everything stored with it on our servers is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email oddwright.support@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my Ironhewn account”. We’ll confirm it’s you and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and email address
  • Your backed-up training data, profile and settings
  • Your coach usage records, consents and any reports you sent

Data is removed from our backups within 30 days. We keep a record of subscription purchase terms where the law requires it (see the Privacy Policy).

Your subscription

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Account → Payments & subscriptions) or the App Store first, so you aren’t charged again.

Data on your phone

Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall Ironhewn or use Settings → Data → Erase all data.
